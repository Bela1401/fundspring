# 3–5 minute demo

1. **Connect (30 seconds).** Open FundSpring, connect a funded EOA, show Arc
   Testnet, USDC balance, and explain that USDC also pays gas.
2. **Create (45 seconds).** Enter a title, HTTPS metadata, goal, deadline, and
   beneficiary. Submit, wait for the final receipt, and open CampaignCreated in
   Arc Explorer.
3. **Contribute (60 seconds).** Enter USDC. With zero allowance, select the
   atomic batch. Show estimated USDC fee, submit once, then show both USDC
   Transfer and ContributionReceived attributed to the wallet.
4. **Memo (45 seconds).** On another contribution with allowance ready, choose
   “With reference.” Show the local reference, final Memo event, and correlated
   activity feed entry.
5. **Settle (45 seconds).** Use an expired demo campaign. Finalize it, then
   demonstrate either the beneficiary claim or one contributor's pull refund.
6. **Close (20 seconds).** Show contract/explorer links and state clearly that
   FundSpring is independent, deployed on Arc Testnet, and unaudited.
